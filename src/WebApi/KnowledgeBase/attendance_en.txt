Attendance and absence reporting (example school policy excerpt)

Reporting an absence
1. Parents or guardians should notify the school through the official parent portal or the attendance office email listed in the welcome pack, as early as possible on the day of absence.
2. For planned absences (medical appointments, family emergencies), supporting documentation may be requested according to the current student handbook.

Late arrival
Students arriving after the morning registration window must sign in at the reception or attendance office so the register can be updated accurately.

Important
Only information published in the official handbook or portal for the current academic year is authoritative. For case-specific decisions, contact the attendance office.
